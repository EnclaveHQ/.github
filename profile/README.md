# EnclaveHQ

**Quiet software for loud nights.**

Enclave runs the boring parts of a lifestyle club so the club can get on with the
interesting parts. Applications, memberships, events, payments, and a private
community, in one place. Discretion is the default, not a feature.

## What Enclave does

- 💳 **Gets you paid when the big processors get nervous.** Payment gateways that are fine with your industry. Memberships, tickets, and payouts included.
- 🪪 **Turns "who are you again?" into a members list.** Applications, tiers, and renewals that run themselves.
- 🎟️ **Retires the door spreadsheet.** Events, tickets, and check-in from a phone.
- 💬 **Keeps the conversation going after the lights come up.** A private community and messaging.
- ✉️ **Reaches the right people, not all the people.** Email campaigns to segments of your members.
- 🤫 **Stays discreet by design.** Privacy first. Always.

## Connect an AI assistant

Enclave publishes an MCP server at `https://api.enclavehq.com/api/mcp`.
It uses OAuth 2.1, so you sign in with your Enclave account and approve the scopes you want.
Your assistant then reads your events, members, and sales, and you keep the keys.
The MCP site has the connect guide and the full list of tools.
